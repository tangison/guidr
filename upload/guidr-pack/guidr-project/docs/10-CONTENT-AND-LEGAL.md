# 10. Content and Legal

## 1. Voice
Warm, plain, specific, calm. See `07-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` section 10. Operators are "guides" or "local operators", never "vendors". Travellers are "you".

## 2. Core messages
- **Tagline options:** "Find your Namibia." / "Guided by people who know the land." / "Book local. Book simply."
- **Promise:** local guides, clear prices, one tap on WhatsApp.
- **Proof:** vetted operators, verified reviews, honest cancellation policies.

## 3. Page copy skeletons

**Home hero:** H1 "Find your Namibia." Sub: "Guided tours and activities from the coast to the canyons, led by local experts." Actions: search; "Plan my trip with Guidr Guide".

**How it works (traveller):** 1 Find an experience. 2 Request your date. 3 Pay the operator by bank transfer or their payment link. 4 Meet your guide. (Short line under each.)

**How it works (operator):** 1 Apply. 2 List your experiences. 3 Get requests on WhatsApp. 4 Get paid directly by travellers; Guidr invoices a small fee twice a month.

**Operator fees page:** state the monthly fee, commission percentage, billing dates (1st and 16th), example calculation, payment methods, late-payment rules (**D3** values).

**Mobile app coming soon:** "Guidr for your pocket is on the way. Join the list and be first to know."

**Footer line:** "Guidr is a marketplace. Tours are provided by independent local operators." plus "Built by Tangison Studio".

## 4. SEO templates
- Experience: `{Title} in {Destination} | Guidr` / description: duration, highlights, "from N$ X", cancellation note.

## 5. FAQ seeds (traveller)
How do I book? How do I pay? Is my payment safe (you pay the operator directly; Guidr never holds your money)? What if the operator cancels? What is the cancellation policy? Do I need a visa **[VERIFY]**? What should I wear and bring? Are tours suitable for children? Can I book for a group? How do I contact my guide? What if I need help during the trip?

## 6. FAQ seeds (operator)
Who can list? What does it cost? When do I get paid (travellers pay you directly)? How do invoices work? What if a traveller does not show up? Can I set my own cancellation policy within presets? How are reviews handled? Can I pause my listing?

## 8. Legal pages: outlines (lawyer must draft and review)

**Terms of use:** who we are; marketplace role (Guidr is not the tour provider); accounts; bookings and payments (traveller pays operator directly); cancellations; reviews; prohibited conduct; liability limits; governing law (Namibia); changes; contact.

**Privacy policy:** data we collect (account, booking, contact, usage, uploaded proof); why; legal bases; who we share with (operators, service providers); international transfers (hosting abroad); retention; security; your rights (access, correction, deletion, objection); children; cookies; contact for requests. **[VERIFY]** Namibian data-protection requirements and GDPR scope for EU visitors.

**Cookie policy:** categories (necessary, analytics, marketing if any); choices; list of cookies.

**Cancellation and refunds:** presets, how refunds work (paid by the operator), disputes, force majeure.

**Operator agreement:** eligibility and licences; accuracy of listings; insurance and safety duties; pricing and fees; commission and invoicing; payment terms; bookings started on Guidr completed through Guidr; conduct and reviews; suspension; indemnity; confidentiality; termination.

**Content policy:** review rules, prohibited content, reporting, moderation.

**Accessibility statement:** commitment, standards, known issues, contact.

**Data requests:** how to export or delete data, response times.

Add a visible line on each legal page: "Last updated: {date}. Draft pending legal review" until reviewed.
