# Message templates


Use `{placeholders}`. Keep WhatsApp messages under 400 characters.

**Booking request to operator (WhatsApp/email)**
"New Guidr request {ref}: {experience}, {date} {time}, {guests} guests. {traveller_name}, {traveller_whatsapp}. Accept or decline within 24 hours: {link}"

**Request received (traveller)**
"Thanks {name}. We sent your request {ref} for {experience} on {date} to {operator}. Most operators reply within a few hours. Track it here: {link}"

**Accepted, payment instructions**
"Good news: {operator} confirmed {experience} on {date}. To secure your spot pay N$ {amount} by {deadline}. Bank transfer reference {pay_ref}, or use the operator's payment link. Details: {link}"

**Proof received (operator)**
"{traveller_name} uploaded proof of payment for {ref}. Please check your bank and confirm: {link}"

**Payment confirmed (traveller)**
"Payment confirmed for {experience} on {date}. Meeting point: {meeting_point}. Your voucher: {link}"

**Payment reminder**
"Reminder: payment for {ref} is due by {deadline}. After that the booking may be released. Pay or message the operator: {link}"

**Trip reminder (48 h / 3 h)**
"{experience} is {when}. Meet at {meeting_point} at {time}. Bring: {bring}. Questions? Message your guide: {guide_whatsapp}"

**Review request**
"How was {experience}? Share a quick review to help other travellers: {link}"

**Declined / expired**
"{operator} cannot take {experience} on {date}. Here are similar options for that day: {link}"

**Cancellation notice**
"Booking {ref} was cancelled by {who}. {policy_outcome}. Details: {link}"

**Invoice issued (operator)**
"Your Guidr invoice {invoice_no} for {period} is ready: N$ {total}. Due {due_date}. Pay by EFT (reference {pay_ref}) or link: {link}"

**Invoice overdue**
"Invoice {invoice_no} is {days} days overdue. New requests are paused until payment. Pay now: {link}"

**Waitlist welcome**
"You are on the Guidr app list. We will message you at launch."
