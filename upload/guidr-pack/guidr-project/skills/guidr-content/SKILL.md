# Guidr Content

Voice and templates: `../../docs/10-CONTENT-AND-LEGAL.md`. Catalogue rules: `../../docs/09-CATALOGUE-AND-PRICING.md`. Design voice: `../../docs/07-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` section 10.

## Voice
Warm, plain, specific, calm. A knowledgeable local friend. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype, no exclamation marks, no "Oops". Operators are "guides" or "local operators". Travellers are "you".

## Formats
- Prices: "From N$ 600 per person". Dates: "Sat 14 Nov". Times: 24-hour ("08:00").
- Buttons are verbs: "Request to book", "Ask on WhatsApp".
- Error: what happened plus what to do next.
- Headline max 8 words; summary max 140 characters; WhatsApp messages under 400 characters.

## Writing a tour listing from notes
1. Extract facts: who, where, when, how long, what is included, price, limits, meeting point, safety.
2. Write: title (max 70 characters, activity first, place second); summary (one sentence, one benefit); description (150 to 400 words, first paragraph answers "what will I do and see"); highlights (3 to 6); itinerary with times; included and not included; what to bring; cancellation preset.
3. Never invent facts. Mark unknowns as **[CONFIRM WITH OPERATOR]**.
4. Do not promise wildlife sightings; say "chance to see".
5. Add accessibility, age and fitness notes.
6. Write alt text for every image: scene plus activity in one sentence.

## Destination and blog posts
Lead with a useful answer, not a greeting. Include practical details (distances, best season, what to book ahead), 3 to 5 local tips, and links to relevant experiences. Verify any factual claim about distances, seasons, fees or rules **[VERIFY]**; cite sources in editor notes.

## Messages
Use templates in `templates/messages.md`; keep placeholders in `{braces}`. Always include the booking reference and one clear next step.

## Legal text
Produce outlines and plain-language drafts only. Mark each with "Draft pending legal review". Never invent legal citations. Flag **[VERIFY]** for Namibian data-protection, consumer and tourism rules, VAT, and WhatsApp/Meta terms.

## Prices and claims
- Prices in demo content are market references; include `checked_on` and source type in the data, and the "Demo price" tag on the page.
- No claims such as "best", "cheapest", "guaranteed" without proof.
- Reviews: never fabricate real-sounding reviews of real businesses; demo reviews are flagged and removed before launch.

## SEO
Experience: `{Title} in {Destination} | Guidr`; destination: `Things to do in {Destination}, Namibia | Guidr`. One H1 per page, descriptive subheads, internal links, FAQ blocks where useful.

## Accessibility of content
Plain language (reading age about 12), descriptive link text, no text in images, expand abbreviations on first use.

## Checklist before delivering
- [ ] Facts verified or flagged
- [ ] Tone matches voice; no exclamation marks
- [ ] Formats correct (price, date, time)
- [ ] Placeholders clear; no real person or business invented
- [ ] Legal text marked as draft
- [ ] Alt text and SEO fields provided
